01 Foundations of Educational Technology & LMS Systems

Explore the historical evolution, architectural principles, and operational infrastructure of modern Learning Management Systems (LMS) such as Canvas, Moodle, and Blackboard.

  • Core LMS standards: SCORM, xAPI (Tin Can), and LTI (Learning Tools Interoperability).
  • Digital content authoring, course structuring, and multimedia integration workflows.
  • Best practices in semantic HTML, CSS styling, and cross-platform responsive design.
02 Pedagogical Design & Digital Learning Accessibility

Master universal design for learning (UDL) frameworks and implement WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility compliance across all digital course modules and interactive assessments.

  • Screen reader compatibility, keyboard navigation, and semantic ARIA landmarks.
  • High contrast color palettes, readable typography, and accessible data visualizations.
  • Cognitive load reduction strategies and asynchronous learning engagement techniques.
03 Learning Analytics & Continuous Quality Assurance

Harness data-driven evaluation pipelines to measure student retention, track milestone completion rates, and proactively identify learners needing additional academic support.

  • Automated quiz evaluation, grading rubrics, and formative assessment analytics.
  • Cohort progression tracking and real-time intervention dashboards.
  • Course iteration protocols based on student feedback and metric thresholds.
